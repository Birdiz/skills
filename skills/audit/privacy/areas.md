# privacy areas and severity

## Areas

- **Inventory and minimisation**: personal data collected, where it is stored and read; data collected with no use in the code; free-text fields that invite sensitive data.
- **Information**: the notice against the processing (purposes, legal bases, recipients, retention periods, rights, contact, transfers); where and when people see it.
- **Legal bases and consent**: a basis for each purpose; consent collected, recorded and withdrawable; terms accepted.
- **Cookies and trackers**: what the browser stores and contacts before any choice, after a refusal; the consent banner; exempt cookies.
- **Third parties and transfers**: recipients, their role, what they receive, contracts, locations and transfer tools outside the EU.
- **Retention and deletion**: periods decided and applied, in every store (tables, sessions, queues, logs, backups); what deletion leaves behind.
- **Rights**: access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection and restriction, and the path a person has to each.
- **Minors and sensitive data**: age rules, parental authorisation and its proof; special categories and data that harms when disclosed.
- **Staff access**: who inside the organisation reads which personal data, against what their role needs; exports.
- **Records and procedures**: record of processing activities, impact assessment, data protection officer, breach procedure.

## Severity grid

What it does to the people, and whose data. "Data that harms" is data of
minors, special categories (health, beliefs, origin...), and data whose
disclosure harms on its own (identity documents, location, finances); a
store holding some of it counts, even mixed with other data.

| | Data that harms | Other personal data |
|---|---|---|
| Used or disclosed with no legal basis (a recipient without contract or basis, a tracker before consent, a purpose people were not told of) | critical | high |
| No limit on what is kept or collected (no retention period applied, data collected with no use), or a right people cannot exercise | high | medium |
| Information missing, or contradicted by the processing | medium | medium |
| Read inside the organisation beyond what a role needs | medium | low |
| A record, contract or procedure missing, no effect on people shown yet | medium | low |
| Good practice beyond the obligation | info | info |

A gap the owner shows is already handled by hand (erasure on request by
e-mail, within the legal delay) moves one level lower. Other moves of one
level: `finding-format`, Rating.
