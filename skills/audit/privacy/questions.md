# privacy questions

What the owner decides and the code cannot show. The axis writes the ones
its documentation does not answer as `Q-PRI-<NN>`, each with the area it
settles and the answer the code suggests; `audit-kickoff` gives this list to
a third-party owner at confirmation.

1. **Law and controller** (all areas): who is the controller (legal entity,
   country), whom the product targets, which law and regulator apply; a
   data protection officer or a contact for privacy.
2. **Purposes and bases** (Legal bases and consent): for each processing
   the inventory lists, its purpose and legal basis.
3. **Retention** (Retention and deletion): for each category, how long it
   is kept once its purpose ends (account closed, event over), and in
   backups and logs.
4. **Recipients** (Third parties and transfers): the processors (hosting,
   e-mail, payment, analytics, support), their location, the contract with
   each, the transfer tool for those outside the EU; other recipients
   (partners, authorities).
5. **Trackers in production** (Cookies and trackers): any analytics,
   advertising or tag manager added at deployment and absent from the
   repository.
6. **Rights** (Rights): how a request is received and handled today, by
   whom, within what delay, and what it reaches (backups, third parties).
7. **Minors** (Minors and sensitive data): the age rules, how parental
   authorisation is obtained and kept; whether health or other special
   data is collected anywhere, free text included.
8. **Staff access** (Staff access): which roles see which personal data,
   and why each needs it.
9. **Records** (Records and procedures): a record of processing
   activities, an impact assessment (done, judged unnecessary, or not
   considered), a breach procedure.
